Connectors allow Prime AI Agent to access and control your other apps directly from your tasks.

**Use cases:**
- Have Prime AI Agent read and respond to your Gmail messages
- Let Prime AI Agent schedule meetings in your Google Calendar
- Enable Prime AI Agent to search and update your Notion databases

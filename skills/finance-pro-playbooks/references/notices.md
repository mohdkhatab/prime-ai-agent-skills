# Relevant Notices

Use only notices relevant to the actual analysis and delivery, adapted to the user's language, structure and length. Preserve the applicable source-license or firm/jurisdiction wording when a specific notice is required; do not invent a universal legal disclaimer.

For investment analysis or recommendations, briefly state the actual data/assumption basis and material uncertainty. Suitable general wording is: “This is research and analysis based on the stated data and assumptions; future performance is not guaranteed, and it is not personalized investment, legal or tax advice.”

For a request to perform a financial transaction, state briefly: “This Skill cannot perform or submit a financial transaction. The response is a proposed, unsubmitted manual plan that you may independently review, change, use or ignore outside the Skill.” Follow [execution-safety.md](execution-safety.md); do not repeat a long notice for a narrow non-transactional concept answer.

# Research Materials Pack

> **Sections**: Ask First · Workflow · Defaults · Pre-Delivery Checks
> **Must not skip**: Defaults · Pre-Delivery Checks — return here before delivery.

> Task `research-materials-pack` in group `deal-materials`. Read [`_overview.md`](_overview.md) first for group-level scope, shared defaults, and format dependencies.

**Use when**: Packaging validated research conclusions into research cards/graphics, roadshow materials, presentation excerpts, social posts, or a materials pack | **Deliverable**: Requested communication materials plus a source list and applicable risk/distribution disclosures

## Ask First
1. Underlying research: upstream `equity-research` / `fund-etf` handoff or user-confirmed conclusions; do not create research conclusions solely to produce materials.
2. Audience and distribution channels (internal, investors, public social media); public release requires pre-release confirmation.
3. Formats and quantity (card set, roadshow deck, single-page summary).

## Workflow
1. Verify research status, data cutoff, and expiration. If expired or `insufficient-data`, return upstream for refresh.
2. Condense conclusions into audience-relevant materials: one core message per card/page, retaining qualifiers, risks, and expiration conditions.
3. Bind every number/fact to the upstream research source and deliver the source list with the pack.
4. Select the requested template and keep external materials in draft until fact, copyright, privacy, audience, and release authorization checks are complete.

## Defaults
- Concise delivery needs only the relevant subject and research context; apply the written source and delivery checks without a full Entity Card or separate validation object.
- Cards contain core conclusion, key data with cutoff date, risk notice, and source note.
- Public-channel materials include a “Not investment advice” disclaimer.

## Pre-Delivery Checks
- Every number is traceable to underlying research; unsupported data is removed or marked unverified.
- Forward-looking statements carry qualifiers and quotations trace to original sources.
- Audience, channel, confidentiality, copyright, and release authorization are confirmed; otherwise retain draft status.

# Deal Materials & Decision Support

Apply [request and delivery priority](../../../output-contracts.md#request-priority) and the [source policy](../../../data-source-policy.md) before task defaults. Provider names, section/page counts, output media, modeling layouts and intake questions in a Playbook are defaults: preserve explicit user choices and answers already supplied. Do not turn a failed lookup into an entity classification or an unsupported estimate into a required number. Accounting identities, truthful evidence, confidentiality and transaction boundaries still apply. For linked models and returns, use the [shared reconciliation rules](../../../metric-dictionary.md#model-reconciliation-and-attribution).

Playbooks for sell-side marketing, capital markets, investment committee, and day-to-day deal-support materials. Task-specific requirements, thresholds, calculations, and handoffs remain in the selected task file.

## Group-Wide Operating Rules

1. **Evidence and gaps.** Use source-supported facts and calculations; distinguish sourced facts, management statements, and analyst assumptions. Do not invent missing facts or metrics: use the task's required placeholder, `TBD`, `NA`, or gap disclosure.
2. **Confidentiality and distribution.** Establish the intended audience, distribution, and confidentiality posture at intake. Apply the selected task's blind/named, project-material, or release controls consistently across visible content, file names, and metadata where applicable.
3. **Decision-useful narrative.** Surface material risks, qualifiers, and open items rather than burying them. Use precise, supportable language; do not pad a document or slide with empty content.
4. **Length and legibility.** Apply a supplied template or explicit length instruction. Absent one, scale the deliverable to the evidence and decision need; paginate or use an appendix when material content cannot be presented legibly. Task-specific layout, density, and minimum-font requirements control.

## Format Dependencies

Load `formats/document_standards.md` for document/PPTX deliverables and `formats/excel_standards.md` for Excel buyer lists or cross-holder workbooks. Task files define their own output package, live-formula requirements, and content ownership; rendering and brand-template handoffs are not a substitute for their required analytical checks.

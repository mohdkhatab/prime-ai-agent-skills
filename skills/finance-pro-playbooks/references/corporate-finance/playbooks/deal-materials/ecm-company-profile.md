# ECM Company Profile

> **Sections**: Ask First · Workflow · Defaults · Pre-Delivery Checks
> **Must not skip**: Defaults · Pre-Delivery Checks — return here before delivery.
> **Formats**: document_standards.md

> Task `ecm-company-profile` in group `deal-materials`. Read [`_overview.md`](_overview.md) first for group-level scope, shared defaults, and format dependencies.

**Use when**: Preparing a pre-meeting company profile, peer benchmarking, or ECM speaking notes for capital-markets, management, or investor meetings | **Deliverable**: Document, presentation, or both, containing a sourced company/financial/peer profile and audience-specific talking points

## Ask First
1. Meeting counterparties and purpose (pre-IPO engagement, follow-on offering, non-deal roadshow, management meeting).
2. Audience (internal team, investment bank, institutional investors) and confidentiality level.
3. Data `as_of` date and any upstream research conclusions or user-verified facts available for reuse.
4. Delivery format: document, presentation, or both.

## Workflow
1. Gather public facts or the upstream `equity-research` handoff and record the data `as_of` date.
2. Organize company overview, financials, peers, capital-markets history (issuances, price performance, shareholder structure), recent controversies, and market focal points.
3. Draft audience-relevant talking points and anticipated Q&A; retain sources, risks, and caveats, and segregate rumors/sell-side viewpoints from facts.
4. Apply the requested template/naming and complete required privacy, copyright, fact, and distribution authorization checks before external release.

For peer benchmarking, use the same fiscal-period convention, currency, units, accounting basis, and metric definitions across every company. If a peer lacks a comparable disclosed metric, show the gap rather than creating a proxy that could mislead the meeting audience.

Identify whether the meeting centers on a primary issuance, secondary liquidity, or investor positioning; frame capital-markets history and anticipated questions accordingly.

## Defaults
- Use 4–6 comparables; apply `metric-dictionary.md`.
- Financial summary: LTM plus the two most recent full fiscal years, with `as_of` shown.
- Keep unverified rumors and sell-side views separate and sourced.

## Pre-Delivery Checks
- Key facts are traceable to public sources or upstream research; unsupported facts are omitted.
- `as_of`, currency, and units are consistent throughout.
- Non-public information has an appropriate privacy classification and audience authorization.

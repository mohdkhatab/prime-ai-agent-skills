# Meeting & Roadshow Minutes

> **Sections**: Audience Adaptation · Input Contract · Workflow · Pre-Delivery Checks · Handoffs
> **Must not skip**: Pre-Delivery Checks — return here before delivery.
> **Formats**: document_standards.md

> Task `meeting-minutes` in group `due-diligence`. Read [`_overview.md`](_overview.md) first for group-level scope and shared operating principles.

**Use when**: Existing buyer-meeting, roadshow, or conference-call recordings, transcripts, or notes need structured minutes and supported quotations.  
**Deliverable**: Audience-appropriate structured minutes with source-supported quotations, commitments, and action items.

## Audience Adaptation

Set depth and format to the stated audience; a concise transcript/minutes treatment still retains full corporate specialist governance. Apply the relevant confidentiality and distribution requirements to the actual source and audience.

## Input Contract

| Field | Requirement | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `subject` | Required | Meeting or roadshow name |
| `source_files` | Required | Recording, transcript, or shorthand notes |
| `audience` | Optional | Internal team, IC, management, or individual |

## Workflow

1. Preserve speaker labels, timestamps, and uncertain-transcript markers.
2. Normalize technical terms and company or product names; mark content that cannot be confirmed.
3. Separate established facts, forward-looking statements, commitments, questions, and action items.
4. Extract quotations only where the original evidence supports verbatim wording.

## Pre-Delivery Checks

- Speaker labels, timestamps, and uncertainty markers are retained.
- Each quotation is locatable in the recording or transcript; otherwise it is a summary.
- Facts, forward-looking statements, commitments, questions, and action items are separately labelled.
- External distribution has the required audience, confidentiality, and copyright authorization.
## Handoffs

- Transfer commitments and action items to `dd-tracker` when ongoing tracking is needed.
- Escalate investment research conclusions to `equity-research`; do not derive investment opinions from minutes alone.
